@title కృదంతాలు · Participles and verbal nouns (कृदन्तम्)
@summary Words made from roots that are not finite verbs: past participles (कृत, कृतवान्), present participles (कुर्वन्, कुर्वाणः), the absolutive and infinitive (कृत्वा, कर्तुम्), gerundives (कर्तव्य, कार्य), and the primary nouns (कर्ता, कर्म, करणम्).
