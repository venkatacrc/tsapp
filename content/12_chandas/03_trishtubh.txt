@title త్రిష్టుప్ · The 11-syllable verse (त्रिष्टुभ्: इन्द्रवज्रा, उपेन्द्रवज्रा, उपजाति)
@summary The longer verses of the Gītā (2.5–8, 2.20–22, 2.29, 2.70, 11.15–50, 15.2–5, 15.15) are triṣṭubh: four quarters of eleven syllables. The two standard patterns indravajrā and upendravajrā, their mixture upajāti, the 12-syllable jagatī, and the freedoms the epic allows.

## Two patterns that differ in one syllable
| Metre | Gaṇas | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| इन्द्रवज्रा | त त ज ग ग | ऽऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ |
| उपेन्द्रवज्रा | ज त ज ग ग | ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ |
| उपजाति | quarters of both kinds mixed | |

> [memory] Indra is heavy, Upendra is light :: Only the first syllable differs: heavy in indravajrā (इन्द्र begins with a heavy syllable), light in upendravajrā (उपेन्द्र begins with a light one). Everything after is the same: ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ. A pause (yati) often falls after the 5th syllable.

| Quarter | Syllables | Pattern | Metre |
|---|---|---|---|
| वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय | वा सां सि जीर् णा नि य था वि हा य | ऽऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ | इन्द्रवज्रा |
| नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि | न वा नि गृह् णा ति न रो प रा णि | ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ | उपेन्द्रवज्रा |
| तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा- | त था श री रा णि वि हा य जीर् णा | ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ | उपेन्द्रवज्रा |
| न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही | न्यन् या नि सं या ति न वा नि दे ही | ऽऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ | इन्द्रवज्रा |

> [note] A quarter can end inside a word :: जीर्णान्यन्यानि (जीर्णानि अन्यानि) is split by sandhi across the 3rd and 4th quarters: the 3rd ends with जीर्णा, the 4th begins with न्यन्यानि. The metre counts sounds, not words. The deck shows the verse with this break.

> [compare] Telugu ఉపజాతి means something else :: In Telugu ఛందస్సు, ఉపజాతులు are తేటగీతి, ఆటవెలది and సీసం (metres built from సూర్య and ఇంద్ర gaṇas). Sanskrit उपजाति is simply a mixed indravajrā / upendravajrā verse. Same word, different meaning.

| अजानता महिमानं तवेदं | अ जा न ता म हि मा नं त वे दं | ।ऽ। ऽ।। ऽऽ। ऽऽ | irregular (epic) |
| मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि | म या प्र मा दात् प्र ण ये न वा पि | ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ | उपेन्द्रवज्रा |

> [warning] The epic is not strict :: About one triṣṭubh quarter in ten in the Gītā departs from the classical patterns (as अजानता महिमानं तवेदं does, or the famous न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन् in 2.20: ।ऽ।ऽ ।।ऽऽ ।ऽऽ). The Gītā is older than the strict classical rules. When a line doesn't scan, it's usually the text, not your scansion.

## Jagatī: twelve syllables
| Metre | Gaṇas | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| वंशस्थ | ज त ज र | ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽ।ऽ | Kālidāsa, Raghuvaṃśa |
| इन्द्रवंशा | त त ज र | ऽऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽ।ऽ | |
| तोटक | स स स स | ।।ऽ ।।ऽ ।।ऽ ।।ऽ | Toṭakāṣṭakam (lesson 12.4) |
| भुजङ्गप्रयात | य य य य | ।ऽऽ ।ऽऽ ।ऽऽ ।ऽऽ | Śaṅkara's Subrahmaṇya-bhujaṅgam |
| स्रग्विणी | र र र र | ऽ।ऽ ऽ।ऽ ऽ।ऽ ऽ।ऽ | Acyutāṣṭakam |

@ex gita:2:5
meter: त्रिष्टुभ् (उपजाति)
note: Quarter 1 गुरूनहत्वा हि महानुभावान्: गु । रू ऽ न । हत् ऽ त्वा ऽ हि । म । हा ऽ नु । भा ऽ वान् ऽ = ।ऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ, upendravajrā. The न of गुरून् joins अहत्वा (गुरून् अहत्वा → गुरूनहत्वा).
note: Quarter 2 श्रेयो भोक्तुं भैक्ष्यमपीह लोके: ऽऽऽ ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ: the 3rd syllable is heavy where indravajrā wants light. Another epic freedom.
@end

@quiz
q: How many syllables in a triṣṭubh quarter?
* 11
- 8
- 12
why: Triṣṭubh = 4 × 11 = 44.

q: Indravajrā and upendravajrā differ in:
* the first syllable only
- the last syllable
- the number of syllables
why: ऽऽ। vs ।ऽ। at the start; the rest is identical.

q: A verse mixing both is called:
* उपजाति
- अनुष्टुभ्
- जगती
why: उपजाति = mixed indravajrā / upendravajrā.

q: The gaṇas of indravajrā:
* त त ज ग ग
- ज त ज ग ग
- म भ न त त ग ग
why: ऽऽ। ऽऽ। ।ऽ। ऽऽ.

q: Why does जीर्णा end a quarter in 2.22?
* metre counts syllables, so a quarter can end inside a sandhi-joined word
- जीर्णा is a separate word
- it is a mistake
why: जीर्णान्यन्यानि spans the 3rd and 4th quarters.
